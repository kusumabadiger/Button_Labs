01 / THE LIBRARY

Meet the button family.

Each button has a job. Choose the right one to guide people through your interface with confidence.

01MOST IMPORTANT

Primary

Highlights the main action on a screen. Use it for the one thing you most want someone to do.

PurposeMain call to actionCommon useSign up, save, continue
02ALTERNATIVE

Secondary

Offers another useful route without competing with the primary action.

PurposeSupporting actionCommon useBack, learn more, cancel
03DESTRUCTIVE

Danger

Signals an action that removes data or cannot easily be undone. Confirmation is recommended.

PurposeDestructive actionCommon useDelete, remove, discard
04POSITIVE FEEDBACK

Success

Communicates completion or approval and gives a clear positive signal.

PurposeConfirm a positive actionCommon useApprove, complete, accept
05CAUTION

Warning

Draws attention to an action that needs care or a decision before proceeding.

PurposeBring attention to riskCommon useReview, renew, caution
06LOW EMPHASIS

Outline

Uses a border instead of a filled background. Useful for secondary actions in a compact layout.

PurposeSubtle actionCommon useFilters, options, alternate paths
07IN PROGRESS

Loading

Shows that the system is working. It helps prevent repeated clicks while a task is processing.

PurposeShow ongoing workCommon useUpload, submit, generate
08UNAVAILABLE

Disabled

Indicates that an action cannot currently be used, often because a requirement is missing.

PurposePrevent unavailable actionCommon useIncomplete forms, locked options
09ICON + LABEL

Icon button

Pairs a symbol with a label to make a common action quick to recognise.

PurposeRecognisable quick actionCommon useAdd, edit, search, share
10TWO-STATE CONTROL

Toggle

Switches between two states. The visual state should always match the current setting.

PurposeSwitch a settingCommon useNotifications, favourites, mute
